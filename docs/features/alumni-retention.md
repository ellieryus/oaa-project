# Mentor Retention, Re-engagement, And Recognition

Last updated: 2026-10-03

**Primary readers:** PM, Backend, Frontend
**Priority:** High

## Purpose

OAA retains Mentors through truthful interest, appreciation, and demonstrated impact, not repeated pressure to respond.

## Request Reminder Policy

- an Ask creates immediate email and inbox notification for the Mentor
- 24 elapsed hours after the Mentor first opens a pending Ask, send at most one reminder
- use `Your Coffee Run is waiting for a response`; do not send an Ask-specific reminder for an Ask that was never opened
- do not send any further Ask-specific reminders after that; normal 48-hour expiry still applies

## Re-engagement And Appreciation

- a Mentor is eligible for non-urgent re-engagement after seven days without activity, subject to low-frequency suppression
- every message must be supported by a real event; do not fabricate Mentee interest or impact
- after self-reporting a conversation happened, the Mentee may send one `THANK_YOU` or `COFFEE_BAG`; it has no monetary value and does not create a messaging thread
- Mentee appreciation is visible only to the participating Mentor

## Post-MVP

- Community Admin metrics, community supply/demand dashboards, and individual activity analytics
- public impact signals, community contributor credential, and badge issuance
- any credential requires verified completion evidence, not self-report alone
