# Pulse Survey And Conversation Capture

Last updated: 2026-10-03

**Primary readers:** Frontend, Backend
**Priority:** Medium

## Core Rules

- MVP pulse invitations are created from Mentee-reported conversation timing, not Calendly provider events
- create at most one optional invitation per participant per eligible Ask
- Mentee feedback asks: `Was this conversation helpful?` and `Did it match what you expected?`
- Mentor feedback asks: `Was the Ask clear and within your scope?` and `Would you accept a similar Ask again?`
- responses are private between participants and do not affect membership, matching eligibility, or Ask history
- Mentee reflection and Mentor private notes are separate, role-protected records
- after self-reporting a conversation happened, a Mentee may send exactly one non-monetary `Thank You` or `Coffee Bag` with optional note up to 280 characters
- appreciation is visible only to the participating Mentor and never exposes private feedback or reflection

## Community Admin Boundary

Community Admins cannot view pulse invitations, responses, reflections, Mentor notes, appreciation notes, or self-reported timing. Community metrics are post-MVP.
