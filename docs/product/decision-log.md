| 2026-07-27 | DEC-001 | Repo-local Markdown docs supersede the external MVP 1 business-rules PDF as the working source of truth. | The technical team needs reviewable, editable documentation in Git. | Feature behavior should now be updated in `features/*.md` and related docs. |
| 2026-07-27 | DEC-002 | Accepted requests use a 24-hour elapsed scheduling window. | The external source had a 12-hour vs 24-hour conflict and the team chose 24 hours. | Superseded by DEC-021. |
| 2026-07-27 | DEC-003 | Matching begins only when the Mentee explicitly selects Start finding mentors. | This preserves deliberate matching activation and aligns with approved MVP behavior. | No automatic matching on profile completion. |
| 2026-07-27 | DEC-004 | MVP 1 uses exactly three active Mentor matches per Mentee batch. | This keeps the experience focused and aligns with approved matching rules. | Matching and replacement logic must preserve slot-based behavior. |
| 2026-07-27 | DEC-005 | Declined Asks are final and cannot be reactivated. | This protects Mentor intent and simplifies lifecycle behavior. | Mentees cannot resend or reopen declined Asks. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-006 | Structured, ranked current priorities are the source of truth for MVP matching. General natural-language Mentor search is deferred. | Structured priorities give Mentees guidance and give Mentors reliable, scannable Ask context. | Mentees select and rank 1-3 current priorities; any future free-text intent must map to editable structured tags and cannot replace this source of truth without evidence. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-007 | Current priorities are dynamic matching state, not long-term Mentee profile help needs. | A Mentee's immediate career needs can change and two parallel priority lists would be confusing. | Initial priorities are seeded from onboarding; Mentees can update them from Profile or Matches, then explicitly confirm whether to update their uncontacted matches. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-008 | Match relevance is explained with human-readable reasons, not a raw score. | Raw scores imply false precision and do not help users understand scope. | The backend retains internal ranking scores; Mentee-facing cards show one personalised reason and Mentor-facing Asks show scoped context. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-009 | Asks snapshot Mentee context at submission. | Mentors must review the intent that existed when the Mentee sent the Ask, even if the Mentee later edits their profile or priorities. | An Ask stores its selected offering, priority snapshot, profile summary, relevant Mentee context, and scoped Ask. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-010 | Surprise Me is deferred until the structured-matching MVP has outcome data. | The beta should first validate clear asks, alumni confidence, and expectation alignment. | Skills, trajectory, certifications, projects, and seniority may be used in future discovery ranking; hobbies remain optional and are not a core matching signal. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-011 | This amends DEC-004: a batch contains exactly three active matches when three qualified candidates exist; otherwise it shows fewer rather than weak or out-of-scope results. | A small beta pool must not be padded with weak or out-of-scope candidates. | The UI explains the smaller set and directs the student to update current priorities. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-012 | Roster-approved alumni may verify an invited personal email; a McGill student email is not required for alumni. | Historical decision. | Superseded by DEC-022 community access and email-ownership model. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-013 | Mentor scope changes apply to future matching only. | Mentors must retain control of current scope without rewriting an Ask already under review. | Existing match and Ask records retain historical context; new Asks validate against current Mentor offerings. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-014 | Decline reasons are Mentee-visible, structured feedback. | Mentees need a clear outcome and OAA needs signal on scope and matching quality. | A final decline records one reason and may include an optional Mentee-visible follow-up message; private internal notes are never exposed. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-015 | OAA does not manage rescheduling or time coordination after an initial booking. | Scheduling changes are better handled directly by the participants in the tools they already use. | Participants coordinate through personal Calendly, LinkedIn, or email; OAA provides no reschedule/cancel UI, workflow, or support. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-016 | Each self-reported conversation offers private feedback for both participants and one Mentee appreciation action. | OAA needs match-quality signals while giving Mentees a lightweight way to acknowledge Mentor time. | Feedback remains private; Mentees may send one non-monetary `Thank You` or `Coffee Bag` appreciation after reporting that the conversation happened. |
| 2026-09-11 | DEC-017 | Mentor retention relies on one Ask reminder, truthful re-engagement, and impact recognition. | Mentors should feel recognized rather than repeatedly pressured to respond. | Ask reminders stop after 24 hours and re-engagement requires a genuine event. The community credential is post-MVP because it requires verified completion evidence. |
| 2026-09-22 | DEC-018 | Alumni eligibility requires manual review of every self-service request against a minimum name-and-cohort-code program roster before personal-email verification. | Historical decision. | Superseded by DEC-022 community membership review; institutional verification is optional per community. |
| 2026-09-22 | DEC-019 | For beta and MVP 1, the backend developer manually reviews every alumni access request through backend/database tooling; a dedicated admin-review UI is deferred. | Historical decision. | Superseded by DEC-024 Community Admin-curated membership. |
| 2026-09-22 | DEC-020 | Student-to-alumni conversion is a future additive flow, not a destructive role switch. | Historical decision. | Superseded by DEC-022 non-exclusive Mentor/Mentee community roles; future role expansion still remains non-destructive. |
| 2026-09-24 | DEC-021 | MVP coordination uses one Mentor-selected Calendly or LinkedIn route at acceptance, not calendar availability gating or verified scheduling. | The beta should preserve high-quality accepted Asks, avoid forcing low-frequency Mentors to create Calendly accounts, and avoid paid integration complexity. | Acceptance snapshots one valid route; accepted Asks do not expire for incomplete logistics. MVP tracks private self-reported conversation signals, while verified completion and the community credential are post-MVP. |
| 2026-10-03 | DEC-022 | MVP is community-enabled: MMA launches first, but CommunityMembership is the authorization boundary and Mentor/Mentee are the product roles. | OAA must support approved communities without tying platform identity to MMA, McGill email, or a global exclusive role. | Superseded in part by DEC-024 for membership acquisition. Cross-community discovery and metrics remain post-MVP. This supersedes MMA-only roster/domain eligibility rules where they conflict. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-023 | MVP has no OAA Admin product role or platform-admin UI. The backend team manually provisions MMA and assigns Community Admins from OAA-approved instructions. | The beta needs one controlled bootstrap path, not another privileged user workflow or administrative surface. | `COMMUNITY_ADMIN` remains a real, community-scoped authorization role. Provisioning records an audit event. Platform-admin capabilities, self-service community creation, and admin assignment are post-MVP decisions. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-024 | MVP uses Community Admin-curated membership, not public join applications. | Members should not discover unrelated communities, and no backend operator should make ordinary eligibility decisions. | Community Admin adds existing verified accounts by exact-email lookup or sends unique email-bound invites to new Mentors or Mentees. Invite acceptance proves ownership of the invited email, so no second OTP is required. `PENDING_REVIEW` and the review queue are removed from MVP. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-025 | An active community membership does not bypass role-profile confirmation. | Existing users can be added to a community before completing relevant data, but matching quality requires complete, reviewed profiles. | Existing role profiles prefill where available; the user must review and confirm them. Mentees cannot match or send an Ask until confirmed; Mentors cannot enter the pool until confirmed and accepting requests. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-026 | A 48-hour non-response releases the Mentee match slot but does not permanently close the Ask to the Mentor. | A late, willing Mentor should be able to respond without taking away the Mentee's replacement opportunity. | The Ask moves to `EXPIRED_AWAITING_LATE_RESPONSE`, appears in Mentor `Expired Asks`, and may become `LATE_ACCEPTED` or `LATE_DECLINED`. The Mentee sees the late decision in Ask history; a late acceptance uses normal coordination handoff. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-027 | A decline does not silently replace a Mentee's match card. | The Mentee should see the reason and choose whether to seek another Mentor, without resetting the rest of their batch. | Declined Ask history and Home show `Find another Mentor`; the action generates one replacement only for that declined slot. Qualifying no-response expiry continues to replace its slot automatically. |
| 2026-10-04 | DEC-028 | MVP participant discovery is directional: Mentees discover Mentors, not other Mentees. | A community membership is an access boundary, not a social directory. Mentee-to-Mentee visibility adds privacy exposure without supporting the MVP matching hypothesis. | No Mentee directory, browse, search, or profile visibility is built. Mentor access to Mentee data is limited to the context required to review and handle a specific Ask. |
